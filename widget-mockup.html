MOMENTUM 4 — home-screen widget

Material Design 1 with rounded corners, in light and dark. Tap the noise switch, AUTO (Adaptive), the slider stops, favorite presets and Bass Boost — visual proposal only; all widgets share one state. Values you change show dimmed until "confirmed".

Noise control has three modes. The switch turns ANC off/on (on restores the last mode). AUTO selects Adaptive ANC — shown explicitly, with no thumb on the slider. Any slider stop selects Custom: ANC on, adaptive off, transparency = 100 − level. Level 100 = full ANC (left, blue), 50 = balanced (hollow ring), 0 = full transparency (right, orange).

EQ: the four buttons are the user's favorite presets (full library and editor in the app). The caption shows what's actually active: preset name, Custom, or the device's audio mode (Podcast, Bypass…). Turning Bass Boost on outside User EQ switches the headphones to User EQ — visible in the caption — and turning it off restores the previous mode.

Unavailable = audio link is up but the control connection failed; the widget doesn't guess which app holds it.
