M4 Control — app

Material Design 1 with rounded corners, light and dark side by side. Both phones are live and share one state — navigate inside either one, or jump with the screen switch.

Home: device status, noise control (switch, Adaptive / Custom, a continuous slider, wind reduction), equalizer with the 4 widget favorites, Bass Boost. Equalizer: sound mode, 5-band editor (−6 to +6 dB), the full preset library — star up to 4 for the widget, save the current bands as a new preset. Settings: optional headphone controls, device info, frame log.

Rules shown: moving the slider selects Custom; editing a band switches to User EQ; turning on Bass Boost outside User EQ switches to it and turning it off restores the previous mode; untested firmware shows a banner.
